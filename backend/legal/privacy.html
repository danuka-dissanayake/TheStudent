4. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. Sessions expire automatically (30 days, extended as you keep using the app). Verification codes are deleted or expire within minutes. When you delete your account, your profile, sessions, codes and attempt records are removed immediately.

5. Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask us to:

  • confirm what data we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your account and data — available directly in the app (Sidebar → Account & Security → Delete account);
  • withdraw consent, for example by deleting your account.

Email [privacy@yourdomain] for any request; we answer within 30 days.

6. Security

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes and never in plain text. Session tokens are stored only as SHA-256 digests on the server, and on your phone they are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS.

7. Children

The app is intended for students aged 13 and above. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian's agreement. If we learn that a child under 13 created an account, we will delete it.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
